# QA Lab Sandbox: Game Design Doc

The sandbox is a small third-person test level used to exercise QA Lab. This doc describes how each feature is **supposed** to work. `qalab triage` retrieves sections of it (RAG) to explain what the expected behaviour was when a bug report is written. Each H2 heading is a feature name; benchmark labels use these exact headings.

## Player movement

The player is a capsule with a CharacterController. Move speed is 4.5 m/s, gravity −20 m/s², jump height 1.2 m. The player can walk on every floor tile and can't pass through walls. If the player ever falls below the kill plane at y = −10, they respawn at the last checkpoint with full health. Component: `SandboxPlayer`.

## Doors

There are three doors (Door_01, Door_02, Door_03). A door opens when the player presses Interact (E) within 2 m and closes again after 4 seconds. Opening rotates the door 90° around its hinge over 0.5 s and plays a creak sound. Doors never block the player permanently, and opening a door must never produce errors. Component: `SeededDoor`, called from `Interactor`.

## Inventory

The inventory has exactly 5 slots (indices 0–4). The HUD shows all 5 slots and refreshes whenever the inventory opens (Tab) or an item is picked up. Requests for a slot outside 0–4 are a programming error; the HUD should only ask for valid indices. Component: `SeededInventory`, displayed by `HudInventory`.

## Enemy registry

Every spawned enemy registers itself with the enemy registry under a unique id (`enemy_<8 hex chars>`) when it spawns, and unregisters when it dies. Any system can look up an enemy by id. Looking up an id must succeed for every living enemy. Ids of dead enemies should be handled gracefully (returns null, no exception). Component: `SeededEnemyRegistry`.

## Spawner

The spawner creates a wave of 3–5 enemies every 10 seconds at its spawn points while the player is within 25 m. It assigns each enemy a target and registers it with the enemy registry. Spawning must work even if a spawn point was removed from the level. Component: `SeededSpawner`.

## Asset loading

Sound effects and textures are loaded on demand from `Assets/Audio` and `Assets/Textures` by name. Every asset referenced by gameplay code must exist in the build. A missing asset should log one clear error with the asset path, not crash, and fall back to a default asset. Component: `AssetLoader`.

## Level geometry

The level is a 40 × 40 m grid of 2 m floor tiles (T_00 to T_399) surrounded by walls. Every visible floor tile must have a collider so the player can stand on it. Corridors and doorways are at least 1.5 m wide, so the player (radius 0.4 m) never gets stuck. Two side rooms: the south-east room (x 30–40 m, z 2–10 m) is reached through a 2 m corridor along the south wall (tiles T_15 to T_19); the north-west room (x 0–10 m, z 30–40 m) through a 1.6 m doorway in its south wall. The NavMesh is baked from render meshes with agent radius 0.3 m. Component: level layout generated by `SandboxSceneBuilder`.

## Performance

Target: 60 fps on a mid-range laptop at 1280×720. Frame time must stay below 33 ms, with no single frame above 50 ms during normal play. Gameplay code must not allocate large amounts of garbage per frame. Pool objects instead. Zones that simulate heavy work (GcZone) exist only to test detectors.

## Art assets

All props (crates, barrels, walls) use the shared `M_Prop` material family. No renderer may be missing its material; Unity shows missing materials as bright magenta, which must never appear in a build. Component: prop prefabs in `Assets/Sandbox/Prefabs`.

## Camera

A third-person follow camera stays 6 m behind and 2.5 m above the player and always renders the world. Camera trigger zones may change framing, but must never stop the 3D view from rendering. The screen should never go black during gameplay except during the 0.3 s fade on respawn. Component: `FollowCamera`, `CameraZone`.

## HUD

The HUD shows the score (top-left), the inventory (bottom), and the ammo icon with count (bottom-right). Text must always fit inside its panel at every score up to 999,999; long values shrink or abbreviate (e.g. 12.3K). Every HUD image must have a sprite; empty images are a bug. Component: `HudScore`, `HudAmmo`.

## Audio

Footstep sounds play based on the surface under the player (Grass, Gravel, Metal, Wood). Every surface type must have a footstep clip. A missing clip should be reported once per surface, not on every step. Component: `FootstepAudio`.

## Combat math

Damage and movement-speed calculations use `MathUtil.SafeDivide(a, b, context)`, which must never be called with b = 0. Weapons always have a fire interval > 0 and movement modifiers always have a duration > 0. A zero divisor means invalid data in a weapon or modifier asset. Components: `DamageCalculator`, `SpeedModel`, `MathUtil`.

## Settings menu

The main menu has Play, Settings, Credits and Quit. In Settings, players change volume and resolution, then press Apply to save and apply them, or Back to cancel. Apply must work from any state, including when nothing was changed. Component: `SeededSettingsMenu`.

## Ballistics range

A test range with a projectile launcher and a 5 cm thin wall. Projectiles travel at 120 m/s and must collide with the wall every time. Fast projectiles should use continuous collision detection so they can't pass through thin colliders. Component: `ProjectileLauncher`, `Projectile`.
